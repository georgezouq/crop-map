Understand the data

Whole-field selection uses USDA CSB 2015–2022 across the contiguous 48 states. These are inferred crop boundaries, not ownership parcels, and stay fixed when you change the crop year. CDL is satellite-classified land cover, not farm declarations or official acreage statistics.

Contiguous 48 states: full coverage since 2008; selected states only in 1997–2007. Hawaii: since 2023. Alaska: not covered by this source.

Native resolution is 10 m from 2024 and 30 m for 2008–2023 in the contiguous U.S. Consider resolution and classification changes when comparing years.

These are currently available historical releases, not snapshots of what was known at the time. They do not, by themselves, prevent look-ahead bias in backtests.

Official data is loaded on demand and cached locally for up to 7 days. Unvisited tiles require a connection; the cache is not a complete national download.
